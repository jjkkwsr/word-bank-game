The weather changes every day! On Monday, it is [sunny] and hot, and the sky is blue. On Tuesday, a white [cloud] appears. By Wednesday, it becomes very [cloudy] and dark. On Thursday, it gets cold and we hear thunder—a big [storm] is coming! There is a strong [wind] blowing. Friday is very [windy], and my hat flies away! On Saturday, it starts to [rain] hard, and we stay inside. On Sunday, it is very cold and white [snow] falls from the sky. We can build a snowman!
